# Decisive go-to-market test: monthly bill control for manpower contractors

Prepared 27 September 2026. This is a proposed market test. It supersedes the default recommendation in [the broad pivot review](./accounting-pivot-options-2026-09-27.md) by choosing one niche, buyer, offer, sales path and ad sequence. It does not authorize public claims or establish demand. No prospect was contacted and no advertising was purchased.

## Method and decision

I applied the niche, dream-outcome, value-equation, grand-slam-offer, lead-magnet, warm-outreach, core-four, content-engine and money-model skills from [zacker-tech/hormozi-skills](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/tree/main/skills) (commit `44c8382`), and market-research, offer, audit, pricing, pitch, hooks, objections, landing-page and time-to-value skills from [alexsmedile/hormozi-skills](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/tree/main/skills) (commit `25ec2b0`). These are third-party adaptations of Alex Hormozi's frameworks. The recommendation below is my decision from those frameworks and cited evidence; it is not a decision or endorsement by Alex Hormozi.

**Choose:** Indian security and housekeeping contractors with 50–300 deployed workers and 3–20 recurring corporate or public-sector buyer accounts. The owner or finance head buys. An accounts executive assembles monthly bills. The buyer's site manager and accounts team approve or block them. The employee and account counts are recruiting filters for this test, not measured market cutoffs.

**Category:** monthly bill readiness and payment-status control for manpower contractors. The working product name is **BillReady**; check rights and buyer comprehension before using it publicly. The accounting app's invoice, receipt, party and file models can be reused, but Tally or the buyer's current books remain the system of record for the first pilot. [Current product scope](../product.md#scope).

**Single promise:** "Know which monthly bills are ready, which the buyer accepted, and which need action before they hold up payment." Do not promise that buyers will pay sooner. Supplier software cannot fix buyer cash shortages or payment policy. [Delayed Payments Report, causes and buyer behaviour](https://www.dnb.co.in/file/reports/Delayed-Payments-Report.pdf).

## Why this niche

The broader [delayed-payments study](https://www.dnb.co.in/file/reports/Delayed-Payments-Report.pdf) estimates material MSME cash tied up in late payment and names invoice submission, documentation, reconciliation and disputes among reported causes. Its interview evidence is from 2022, so it motivates a current buying test rather than proving today's conversion. A [2025 government contract](https://www.igcar.gov.in/tenders/IGC_TS_MISC_2025_16431.pdf) requires a contractor to pay wages soon after month-end and attach attendance, wage-transfer and statutory-payment proof to its bill. A [2025 security-services contract](https://ccsniah.gov.in/contract/2025/Security_GEMC_511687771948914_2025.pdf) ties payment to a valid, complete invoice. These documents show a concrete repeated workflow, not that every buyer uses the same checklist. The [Department of Expenditure's 2025 procurement manual](https://doe.gov.in/files/circulars_document/MfPoNCS_2025.pdf) discusses bill-submission dates and payment-status tracking for service contractors.

This is a contested market. [StaffingGo](https://staffinggo.in/pricing) sells contractor software with client, PO, timesheet, payroll, billing and payment features from ₹60 per consultant per month in one listed plan. [Secura Plus](https://securaplus.in/pricing) lists attendance, billing and statutory reports for manpower agencies. [Credflow](https://credfloat.in/pricing) sells reminders and collection tools for Tally/Busy users. The first sales call must find a narrow workflow those products do not already solve well for the prospect. Competitor feature pages prove what is offered, not what users achieve.

## Hormozi value-equation diagnosis

| Driver        | Decision for this offer                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Dream outcome | A complete monthly bill reaches the buyer on time; the contractor knows its acceptance and expected payment state before the next payroll cycle.                                                                               |
| Likelihood    | Review a real prior bill packet and show its missing items and timeline. Obtain written buyer requirements. Never imply a sent email equals acceptance.                                                                        |
| Time delay    | Deliver a one-bill diagnostic within two business days of receiving redacted material. Run the first paid sprint on the next billing cycle. The timing is a proposed service target that must be measured in the first pilots. |
| Effort        | The team sends its existing contract, PO, attendance and bill records once. We build the checklist and status board with them. Do not demand a full accounting migration.                                                      |

The weakest driver is **likelihood**: no buyer has paid or shown that this focused layer beats its current process. The first win is a precise status and missing-item list for one actual bill. A larger promise, more bonuses or a fabricated value stack cannot repair that proof gap. [Value equation](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/value-equation/SKILL.md); [offer audit](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/audit-offer/SKILL.md).

## Offer and money model

**Lead magnet — one-bill delay diagnosis:** The prospect provides a redacted monthly bill, the buyer's submission requirements, and the last buyer response. In a 20-minute call, mark the date the work ended, the date the complete packet was submitted, the date the buyer acknowledged it, the blocker, and the next owner. Return a one-page timeline. This is a narrow, useful deliverable, not a promise to collect money. Ask permission before receiving sensitive wage or employee data. [Lead-magnet skill](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/lead-magnet/SKILL.md).

**Core paid offer — 30-Day BillReady Sprint:** ₹19,900 for one monthly cycle, up to 10 buyer/site bill packets, one setup call, buyer-specific checklists, a shared status board, one weekly exception review, and a final handover with submitted, acknowledged, blocked, promised and paid statuses backed by evidence. The fee, cap and scope are a **price experiment**. They are not market facts. Exclude payroll processing, tax advice, buyer negotiations, legal recovery and a guaranteed payment date. Pilot capacity: at most two simultaneous customers while this is done with the customer.

**Guarantee:** If we fail to deliver the agreed checklist, status board and weekly reviews despite timely access to the agreed records, refund the pilot fee. Do not guarantee buyer acceptance or cash collection. Confirm exact terms with the buyer before taking money. A narrow delivery guarantee reverses controllable risk without promising what a third party controls. [Offer skill](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/hormozi-offer/SKILL.md).

**Continuation:** Quote ₹7,900 per month for the software board plus one weekly exception review only after measuring pilot hours and renewal demand. An additional buyer account, site or company is scoped and priced in writing, with no hidden usage cap. If manual service hours make the plan unprofitable, raise the price or remove the review; do not assume software automation will erase the work. [Money-model skill](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/money-model/SKILL.md); [Credflow price anchor](https://credfloat.in/pricing).

**No stacked bonus values:** The only included assets are a buyer-specific packet checklist, a submission/acceptance timeline, and a handover export. Listing invented rupee values for these would overstate evidence. They address the buyer's three objections: missing proof, unclear buyer state, and lock-in to another system. [Bonus-stack skill](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/bonus-stack/SKILL.md).

## Positioning, objections and pitch

**Positioning line:** "BillReady helps security and housekeeping contractors prepare complete monthly bill packets and track buyer acceptance while they keep their existing accounting software."

| Objection                                    | Answer to test in calls                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "We already have Tally or StaffingGo."       | "Keep it. Show us the last bill that waited. If your current system already shows every required document, buyer acknowledgement and blocker in one place, you do not need this." |
| "The buyer is late because they lack money." | "We cannot change that. The sprint will separate buyer-side delay from a packet we can correct and record the payment commitment."                                                |
| "This is too much work for my team."         | "Use your existing files. We set up the first buyer checklist and status board together. The test measures whether it saves more work than it adds."                              |
| "Can you handle PF/ESI and compliance?"      | "We track whether the buyer's required evidence is present. Your payroll or compliance professional remains responsible for the figures and legal correctness."                   |

**15-second pitch:** "Monthly bills for security and housekeeping contracts often wait on attendance, wage proof or a buyer acknowledgement. We organize each buyer's packet and show exactly what is ready, blocked or promised for payment. You keep Tally."

**Sales-call script:**

1. "Take me through the last bill that was not paid when expected. What service month and buyer was it?"
2. "On what dates were attendance approved, wages paid, the bill submitted, and the bill acknowledged? Show me the messages or portal status if possible."
3. "What exact document or decision blocked it? Who could fix that? What did it cost your team in follow-up hours or working capital?"
4. "What does Tally, StaffingGo or your spreadsheet already show? Where do you go to see buyer acknowledgement and the next action?"
5. "If we track the next 10 monthly packets together for 30 days, what would make the sprint worth paying for? Who approves this purchase?"
6. Present the scoped ₹19,900 pilot only when the workflow shows repeated supplier-fixable blockers. Ask for a dated start and payment. Record a no as a no; do not count praise as demand.

**Follow-up after a qualified call:** "You showed three points where last month's bill waited: [observed facts]. The 30-day sprint would track up to 10 buyer packets, assign each missing item, and record buyer acknowledgement and payment commitments. The fee is ₹19,900 for one cycle. If you want to test it on [month], I will send the exact scope and data-handling terms." Fill the brackets only from the call. No outreach is sent by this document.

**Follow-up sequence:** Send the factual recap on the call day. After two business days, ask one question: "Is the next monthly billing cycle the right one for this test?" After seven days without a reply, send the one-page diagnostic and close the loop: "I will leave this here. If the same blocker appears next month, you can reply with that bill's date." Stop there unless the prospect responds. The sequence is a proposed operating rule, not a claim about optimal timing.

## Acquisition: one channel before four

**Primary channel, first 30 days:** founder-led one-to-one outreach and referrals to owners or finance heads of security and housekeeping contractors. Start from existing warm contacts, then a researched list of firms with recurring corporate or public-sector service contracts. Do not scrape private contact data or send bulk automation. The [Core Four](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/core-four/SKILL.md) exercise favors direct conversations at this stage because the offer has no proof and the buyer count is small.

**Initial outreach draft:** "I saw that your firm provides security/housekeeping teams to multiple sites. I am studying the monthly gap between work completed, bill accepted and payment released. Could you show me one delayed bill with client and employee details hidden? I will map its timeline and missing steps in a 20-minute call." Personalize only what is verified. Do not say every contractor has the problem.

**Activity test:** build a list of 60 qualified firms, send 15 individually researched approaches each week for four weeks, and request referrals from five relevant accountants or contractors. Track reply, call, shown bill, qualifying blocker, paid pilot and reason for no. These are operating targets, not expected conversion rates. Stop or revise the message if no one will show a real bill after the first 20 approaches. [Warm outreach skill](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/warm-outreach/SKILL.md).

## Social media handling

**One platform:** LinkedIn, from the founder's account. A company page can hold the product facts, but the founder leads the conversation. Do not buy followers or publish testimonials without permission. The goal is conversations with contractors, not reach for its own sake. [Content-engine skill](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/content-engine/SKILL.md).

**Weekly system:** one documented, permissioned bill-packet lesson; turn it into three short posts: (1) one exact delay and its timeline, (2) a checklist item that prevented a rejection, (3) a buyer-process question. Before case permission exists, use clearly labelled contract examples or public procurement requirements, not fictional customer wins. Publish Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday as a four-week operational test; timing is an experiment, not a performance claim.

**First three hooks:**

- "Your monthly bill can be on time and still be incomplete. Who confirms the buyer accepted it?"
- "Security contractors: the invoice date is only one date. Track attendance approval, complete submission and buyer acknowledgement too."
- "Before chasing payment, find out whether the buyer's accounts team has accepted the bill packet."

**Four-week content plan:**

| Week | Main lesson                                                                  | Three posts cut from it                                                                                                            |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Break down one public contract's monthly bill requirements.                  | Required proof; submission date versus acceptance date; one question for contractors.                                              |
| 2    | Show a blank, buyer-specific bill-packet checklist.                          | How to read a PO; who approves attendance; what counts as acknowledgement.                                                         |
| 3    | Show one permissioned and anonymized bill timeline, if a prospect allows it. | Missing item; time spent waiting; the supplier action that was possible. If no permission, use a labelled public-contract example. |
| 4    | Explain the paid sprint's actual process and limits.                         | First-day setup; weekly exception review; what the service cannot make a buyer do.                                                 |

**Example first post:** "A monthly bill has at least three dates: the service month ended, the contractor sent the packet, and the buyer accepted it. A [public 2025 service contract](https://www.igcar.gov.in/tenders/IGC_TS_MISC_2025_16431.pdf) also requires attendance and wage-payment evidence with the bill. If your dashboard shows only the invoice date, which date tells you whether the buyer can process it?" The post uses a public example, not an invented customer result.

**Comment reply:** Answer the specific question in public, cite the contract requirement or evidence, and invite a private, redacted example only if the person asks for help. **DM reply to interest:** "Happy to map one delayed bill. Please hide employee and buyer identifiers before sharing. The call takes 20 minutes; I will return the timeline and next action." Do not make legal or compliance claims in replies. [Objection skill](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/objection-destroyer/SKILL.md).

## Ads: prepared, gated, measurable

**Do not run ads first.** Start them after three paid sprints, at least two renewals or referrals, one permitted case timeline, and measured contribution margin. The first 30 days should buy learning through direct sales. Then test one Google Search campaign, not broad Meta or LinkedIn awareness ads. Search intent can match a specific bill-packet problem; this is a channel hypothesis. [Core Four skill](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/core-four/SKILL.md).

**Campaign proposal:** India; search terms around `manpower bill documentation`, `security agency billing software`, `contractor invoice tracking` and `housekeeping billing software`. Exclude job seekers, payslips, payroll calculators and generic accounting courses. Use one landing page and one conversion: a qualified bill-review booking. A proposed test cap is ₹20,000 across 14 days. Pause if traffic does not produce a qualified bill review; calculate allowable acquisition cost from _measured_ pilot margin before spending further. No benchmark CPC or conversion rate is assumed.

**Search ad draft:** headlines `Monthly Bill Status` and `Security Bill Pack Review`; description `Track required proof, buyer acknowledgement and blocked bills. Keep your books in Tally.` CTA: `Review one delayed bill`. Google currently permits up to 30 characters per responsive-search headline and 90 per description; verify the final assets in the ad interface. [Google Ads specifications](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17092074?hl=en-GB).

**Second ad angle to test:** headlines `Manpower Bill Checklist` and `Track Buyer Acceptance`; description `See missing bill documents and buyer responses for each monthly contract. Book a review.` The two angles test packet completeness against status visibility. Do not interpret clicks as demand; count only qualified bill reviews and paid sprints.

## Scoreboard and stop rules

The first 30 days seek **12 real bill reviews, 3 offers made and 2 paid sprints**. These are decision thresholds, not forecasts. Record the source and dates of each bill, reason for the delay, whether supplier action could change it, existing software, quote, answer, pilot hours, accepted packets, buyer responses, and any renewal. Never attribute a buyer's payment to this product without a defensible comparison.

**Execution order:** Week 1, assemble the 60-firm list and run the first five bill reviews. Week 2, run seven more reviews and issue only qualified offers. Weeks 3–4, deliver at most two paid pilots, measure hours, and ask for continuation. Social posts document the actual learning. The landing draft can be used privately during discovery. Search ads stay gated by the proof and margin conditions above.

Proceed to build the first software slice if two paid pilots reveal the same supplier-fixable status or documentation gap and at least one buyer renews or refers a peer. Stop or change the offer if the blockers are mostly buyer cash shortages, client policy, or work already handled well in an incumbent. If no one will pay for the manual sprint, do not build its automation. These gates operationalize the [market-research](https://github.com/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/market-research/SKILL.md) and [money-model](https://github.com/zacker-tech/hormozi-skills/blob/main/skills/money-model/SKILL.md) skills.

## Evidence boundary

Public contracts prove that complete bill packets and fixed wage dates can matter in this niche. Competitor pages prove that alternatives exist. The 2022 study supports the broad delayed-payment problem and possible causes. None proves that BillReady is differentiated, that the stated price will clear, or that marketing copy will convert. Those facts require observed bill chains and paid pilots.
