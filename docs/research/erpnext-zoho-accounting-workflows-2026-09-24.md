# ERPNext and Zoho Books accounting workflows against Accly's slices

Date: 2026-09-24. ERPNext repository evidence is pinned to [`version-15` commit `4aee12e`](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/tree/4aee12e16c664897571457c007aaa95b8364bbbb). ERPNext and Zoho help pages are live documentation read on this date. Zoho Books evidence uses the India edition where available.

## Question

Across Accly Books accounting-core slices 1–5 and the planned slices, do ERPNext and Zoho Books support our practical workflows? Where do our choices add needless concepts, and where does a missing step prevent a user from completing the accounting job?

## Answer

**Keep the document-first core and its separate sales, purchase, money, journal, opening, and settlement flows.** Both references use that operator sequence. The differences below have distinct roadmap states. Reports, import and party journal settlement are already specified as future slices. TDS thresholds, the first deposit workflow and card clearing are explicitly deferred until named pilot cases. Combining payment mode with the bank account is a current design choice, not an unfinished slice. These findings do not call for a generic transaction engine. [Accly contract](../specs/accounting-core.md#slices), [deferred work](../specs/accounting-core.md#deferred), [work registry](../README.md#work-lifecycle), [ERPNext Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry), [Zoho Payments Received](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/payments-received/basic-functions.html)

The references validate _workflow shape_, not our pilot's speed or statutory correctness. Accly's CA acceptance, measured entry-speed gate, and real cutover file remain the decision tests. [Product gates](../product.md#position), [work registry](../README.md#work-lifecycle)

### The payment-method question

ERPNext's Mode of Payment is a defaulting aid, not the sole source of the bank account. Its Payment Entry carries **Receive/Pay/Transfer** and **Paid From/Paid To** separately. The pinned v15 mode schema has no Receive/Pay direction flag. [Mode docs](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/mode_of_payment), [mode schema](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/4aee12e16c664897571457c007aaa95b8364bbbb/erpnext/accounts/doctype/mode_of_payment/mode_of_payment.json#L9-L44), [Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry)

Zoho Books shows **Payment Mode + Deposit To** for receipts and **Payment Mode + Paid Through** for supplier payments. These are user-visible separate choices. [Payments Received](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/payments-received/basic-functions.html), [vendor payment](https://www.zoho.com/kw/books/help/payments-made/vendor-payments.html)

Accly currently stores `name + accountId + active` on one Payment Method. Receipt, Payment and paid-now Invoice use the same shared field and active-method list. The demo seed names rows `HDFC NEFT`, `HDFC card machine`, and similar account/rail combinations. Thus a receipt-only card machine appears on Payment. `packages/db/src/schema/payment-methods.ts:14-26`; `apps/web/src/components/payment-method-field.tsx:17-58`; `apps/web/src/components/receipt-form.tsx:408`; `apps/web/src/components/payment-form.tsx:446`; `apps/web/src/components/invoice-form.tsx:758`; `scripts/seed.ts:87-99`

**Inference and candidate to test:** ask for the actual **cash/bank account** and a small **mode** (cash, transfer, UPI, cheque, card machine), with a mode-specific reference. Preselect the likely account to preserve speed. The receipt and payment screens may present different mode choices, but that filtering is our UX decision, not an ERPNext feature claim. Compare the extra field against the current combined picker on a real invoice clearance, supplier payment, and card receipt before changing the schema. [ERPNext Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry), [Zoho receipt](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/payments-received/basic-functions.html), [Zoho vendor payment](https://www.zoho.com/kw/books/help/payments-made/vendor-payments.html), [Accly speed gate](../specs/client-patterns.md#speed-gate-h4)

### Risks that deserve an owner before optional features

1. **TDS correctness.** Selecting a section currently applies its rate to the entered amount after date and PAN checks; the spec explicitly defers thresholds, overrides and no-PAN rates. ERPNext has threshold logic, and Zoho supports TDS on bills and a later liability-to-challan flow. A ₹5,000 payment alone does not establish that rent TDS is due; the cited 2025 Act table lists a ₹50,000 monthly threshold for the selected rent category. Require a CA-reviewed case before treating the screenshot's ₹500 as a compliant deduction. `packages/api/src/routers/payment.ts:141-177`; `docs/specs/accounting-core.md:800-808`; [ERPNext withholding](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/tax-withholding-category); [Zoho challans](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/tds/tds-challans.html); [Income Tax Act, section 393](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf)
2. **A card receipt's bank date.** The template posts Card directly to Bank Account even though its own comment describes a later net payout. A card clearing account is deferred until bank balances must match daily. Do not claim same-day bank accuracy for those receipts before testing a real statement. `packages/api/src/core/chart-templates.ts:40-53`; `docs/specs/accounting-core.md:785-790`; [Zoho banking workflow](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/banking/)
3. **Migration and statements.** Open items, advances and a control-account balance must reconcile at cutover. Both references guide users through opening transactions and balances; our import and reports are open. Validate one entity's source Trial Balance, party outstanding and bank balances end to end. `docs/specs/accounting-core.md:473-499`; [ERPNext opening](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/opening-and-closing); [Zoho migration](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/migration/migrating-to-zoho-books-from-other-software.html)

## What this proves and does not prove

The cited documentation establishes the visible vendor workflows. The pinned ERPNext v15 source additionally establishes mode-account defaulting, the lack of a direction field in its mode schema, payment account roles, withholding threshold branches, and party validation on journal rows. ERPNext's live docs may describe behavior newer than that commit. Zoho's source and production organization were not inspected, so its internal ledger shape, exact API semantics and every regional variant are unknown. [ERPNext pinned tree](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/tree/4aee12e16c664897571457c007aaa95b8364bbbb), [Zoho Books help](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/)

Neither vendor's feature list proves adoption, speed, usability in our pilot, the correctness of our tax seed, or the need to implement its gateways, approvals, inventory, multicurrency, or bank feeds. Our product keeps those evidence-gated. [Product position and scope](../product.md#scope), [CA interview protocol](../validation/ca-interviews.md)

## What this means for us

- **Keep:** separate Invoice, Bill, Receipt, Payment, Note, Journal and Opening Balance ownership; explicit allocations and reversals; a compact chart and scoped accounts. Those are supported by the comparable operator flows and already have one local posting spine. `docs/specs/accounting-core.md:9-14,224-464`; [ERPNext Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry); [Zoho Bills](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/bills/basic-functions.html)
- **Test before changing:** separate mode and money account in Receipt/Payment. The present combined picker saves one field, but confuses bank identity with transfer rail and offers card machine for an outgoing Payment. Measure it against the pilot's entry-speed gate before choosing a schema change or a smaller direction-specific filter. `packages/db/src/schema/payment-methods.ts:14-26`; `apps/web/src/components/payment-method-field.tsx:17-58`; [ERPNext mode](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/mode_of_payment); [Zoho receipt](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/payments-received/basic-functions.html)
- **Follow the existing plan:** slices 6, 7 and 9 own reports, cutover import and party-journal settlement. The TDS threshold and deposit decisions remain gated on their first real pilot cases; do not present the current deduction screen as a full TDS compliance workflow. `docs/specs/accounting-core.md:473-499,560-664,800-808`; [work registry](../README.md#work-lifecycle); [Zoho challans](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/tds/tds-challans.html)
- **Keep gated:** gateway, bank reconciliation, inventory, recurring/approval workflows, multi-currency and broad report customization. The references show these as available capabilities, but our product's evidence gates still own their start. [Product scope](../product.md#scope); [ERPNext bank reconciliation](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/bank-reconciliation); [Zoho banking](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/banking/)

## Next falsification

With the pilot CA, enter the same six real cases in Accly and a reference product: invoice clearance across two invoices; customer advance later applied and partly reversed; card receipt whose bank payout is net of fees; supplier Bill with TDS followed by government challan; a supplier credit against a paid Bill; and a cutover with open invoices, bills and an advance. Capture required fields, bank and party balances, tax liability, reports, correction steps and entry time. A case that cannot be represented without a manual unexplained Journal falsifies the corresponding current workflow.
