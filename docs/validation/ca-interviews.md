# CA interviews and month-end observation

The external-launch gate in [Product](../product.md#position). Five CA firms, 30 to 40 minutes each, plus one observed month-end per firm where possible. It scores H1 (the CA–client exchange costs real time each month), H2 (CAs want live shared books, not exports) and H7 (a CA firm is a working channel). Ask about the last time something happened, never whether they would use or pay. Record behaviour and artifacts, not opinions.

## Who to recruit

- Five practising CA firms with 15 or more GST clients, at least two clients in the 2 to 50 crore band, and their own staff doing 2B and IMS work.
- Avoid friends who will be polite. Prefer firms reached through a cold referral from a client, since that is the channel being tested.
- For two of the firms, also interview the in-house accountant at one client (15 minutes, section 6).

## Opening (2 minutes)

We are studying how CA firms and their clients do monthly books and GST together. No product pitch. Ask for permission to record, and for permission to look at real artifacts on screen with client names hidden.

## 1. Last month-end for one client (10 minutes)

Pick one client in the 2 to 50 crore band. Walk the most recent GSTR-3B cycle step by step.

- What arrived from the client, in what form, on which day? (Tally backup, Excel, PDFs, WhatsApp photos, portal login.) Ask to see the actual file or chat.
- What did your staff do first with it? How long before it was usable?
- How many times did you go back to the client with questions? What was the last question? How long until the answer?
- Where did 2B reconciliation happen, in which tool, and who clicked? Same for IMS actions.
- What was the last thing that was wrong in the client's data? How did you find it, and how was it fixed on the client's side?
- On what date did you file, against what due date? What delayed it?

Record: elapsed days from data received to filed, hours of staff effort, number of client round trips, tools touched.

## 2. Tools and handoffs (5 minutes)

- Which Tally release and TSS status do most clients run? Do any use Connected GST features inside Tally? Which ones?
- Any clients on Zoho Books, Busy, Marg, Vyapar? What happened when one moved?
- Which reconciliation or filing tool does the firm itself pay for (ClearTax, Suvit, TaxSolver, IRIS, other)? Annual cost? What does it do that Tally does not?
- Show me the firm's own tracker for deadlines across clients (Excel, WhatsApp group, tool). Ask to see it.

## 3. The last correction after filing (5 minutes)

- Tell me about the last time a client changed a booked entry after GSTR-3B was filed. What happened to the return and to the ledger?
- Do you set a back-dated entry cut-off in the client's Tally? Who owns that setting?
- When an auditor asked who changed what, how did you answer? Show the log if there is one.

## 4. Multi-client oversight (5 minutes)

- On the 18th of the month, how do you know which clients are not ready? Show the view.
- How many clients per staff member? What breaks first when a staff member leaves?
- What is the one thing you check across all clients every week by hand?

## 5. Tally lock-in and migration (5 minutes)

- Last time a client changed accounting software, who decided, and why? What broke during migration?
- If a client's books lived in the cloud with your firm having a login, what would you stop doing? What would you refuse to do?
- Would you want the client to see the same screens as your staff, or a read-only view, or nothing until you publish? Ask them to describe the last time a client saw work in progress and what happened.

## 6. In-house accountant (15 minutes, two clients)

- Show me the last invoice you entered. Time a new five-line invoice on the current setup; count keystrokes and mouse use.
- What do you send the CA, when, and how? What comes back?
- What is the last thing the CA asked you to change? How long did it take?
- What happens when the internet is down for a day?

## 7. Money and authority (3 minutes)

- Who pays for the client's accounting software, and who pays for the firm's filing tools? Show the last invoice amounts if comfortable.
- Who would sign off on moving a client's books: the owner, the accountant, or you?

## 8. Commitment ask (2 minutes, the only test of H2)

Ask exactly this: "If we set up a shared workspace with one of your clients' sanitized data next month, would you put it on the calendar now, and which client?"

- A scheduled date with a named client counts as a pass for that firm.
- "Send me details" or "sounds interesting" counts as no.
- Record what they offered to share and any data-handling boundary they set. Use sanitized or synthetic samples by default.

## Pass and kill for the set

- H1 passes if observed or artifact-backed effort is 4 hours or more per client per month at 3 of 5 firms.
- H2 passes if 3 of 5 firms schedule a trial; kill at 0 or 1.
- H7 passes if at least one firm introduces 3 qualified clients within 30 days of the interview.
- Reframe trigger: 3 or more firms prefer publish-only or a CA-only tool over a shared workspace.
