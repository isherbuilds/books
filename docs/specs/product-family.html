Flow

One request, one transaction, one ledger

Shaded boxes are Finance; white boxes are the School module.

Problem

Three apps on one stack are three hand-made copies of the same platform, and the copies already differ. Money is recorded twice: School keeps its own fee ledger, HMS keeps its own ledger, and Books would be a third, fed by exports. The owner waits for a sync, and the CA reconciles two ledgers for one entity.

Solution

One SaaS at one domain (example.com/<org-slug>), one shared database, and one build. Finance (Billing and General Accounting) is always on. School and HMS are modules turned on per Organization. A module posts Finance documents in its own transaction and keeps no money records of its own.

Goal

A School fee collection appears on the Day Book, the party statement and the balance sheet with no sync. A platform fix is made once.

Design direction

Books' design system is the direction: docs/design.md and the tokens in packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css. The mockups copy the real shell, DataTable, inset Sheet and Badge. Ported School screens keep their layout and use Books tokens. School's tone colours (mint, sky, blush) map to Books' four state meanings. Proposed: the Modules section (S1), the School group after Accounting (S2), and the Sheet footer note (S4).

F1 · Module switch (S1, S2)

R1.1 organization_settings.modules is a set drawn from school and hms; empty means Books only.
R1.2 The founder's Create organization form sets the modules. Nothing changes them afterwards. S1
R1.3 member.me returns the modules. The shell shows a module's group only when it is on. S2
R1.4 orgProcedure refuses a module statement when the module is off: FORBIDDEN with MODULE_OFF. The check reuses the same membership read and is audited like a role denial.
R1.5 A route under /$orgSlug/<module>/ returns not-found when the module is off. S2

F2 · Document source (S4)

R2.1 documents.source records user, school or hms.
R2.2 Books refuses to cancel a document whose source is not user (CONFLICT, SOURCE_OWNED, "Cancel this in School."). The Sheet shows "From School" and has no Cancel action. S4
R2.3 The module cancels through the same Finance cancel command, so period locks and allocation rules stay in one place.
R2.4 Reports, statements, allocations and exports treat every source the same.

F3 · School module (S2, S3)

R3.1 Admission creates a customer Party and stores student.partyId. Fee figures are read from the Party. S2
R3.2 A fee head is an Item with an income Account and the GST-exempt tax code.
R3.3 Fee structures stay in School as templates.
R3.4 Generate demands posts one Invoice per enrollment per period, with one line per head. The link row fee_demand(orgId, enrollmentId, periodLabel, documentId) is unique per period, so a rerun skips existing rows. S3
R3.5 Cancelling a demand cancels its Invoice through Finance. A demand with active receipts is refused.
R3.6 A concession posts a Credit Note against the demand line.
R3.7 Collect posts one Receipt against per payment leg (up to four) in one transaction. The total may not exceed the selected outstanding. One slip lists every Receipt number. Split Receipt is deferred. S3
R3.8 Voiding a collection cancels each Receipt through Finance. The correction flag moves to fee_receipt.
R3.9 Ledger, today's collection and defaulters read Finance. School's fee ledger, receipt and counter tables are not ported.
R3.10 admin, frontoffice and teacher join access.ts and authorize as a union with Finance roles. The teacher's section limit ports with School.

Shared rules

  • Every module table has orgId NOT NULL and the tenant predicate. Document references use (orgId, documentId).
  • Modules call only exported (tx, scope, settings, input) Finance commands. postReceipt is the prior art.
  • Finance commands never audit. The module audits after commit.

Out of scope

  • HMS code changes
  • A database per customer, sharding, on-premises installs
  • Offline sync
  • Personal income tax filing
  • Consolidated reports across Organizations

Deferred, with gates

  • HMS module: after the HMS pilot month and Slice 3. HMS Invoice, Payment, advance, refund and credit note map to Invoice, Receipt, Receipt advance, Payment (refund) and Credit Note; payers become Parties. HMS's ledger tables are deleted.
  • Changing modules after go-live: when a live Organization asks.
  • General Accounting switch: when a paying customer keeps Tally.
  • Per-Organization export and restore, and row-level security: before the first outside paying customer.
  • Several payment legs on one Receipt: when the school office reports confusion.
  • External ingestion (externalRef, API keys): post-MVP.

Open decisions

None.

Ownership

  • Home: this repository, @accly/*. School is ported file by file, not git-merged. Its repository becomes read-only after Slice 3. HMS stays in its own repository until its gate.
  • Layout: module directories, not packages:
    • packages/db/src/schema/school/
    • packages/api/src/school/ and routers/school/, mounted as appRouter.school
    • apps/web/src/routes/$orgSlug/school/
    • tests/integration/school/
  • Import direction: no-restricted-imports overrides in .oxlintrc.json, plus a new AGENTS.md enforcement row.

Contracts

  • documents.source: text, not null, default user.
  • organization_settings.modules: text[], not null, default empty. Values are validated in the app.
  • The baseline is regenerated with bun run db:generate and the database reset (hard rule 4).
  • type Module = "school" | "hms", and MODULE_OF maps each statement to its module in access.ts.
  • New Finance commands: postInvoice, postCreditNote and cancelDocument, each (tx, scope, settings|{documentId}, input & {source}). postReceipt gains source.
  • Errors: FORBIDDEN/MODULE_OFF, CONFLICT/SOURCE_OWNED.
  • General Accounting always posts. There is no switch.

Test seams

  • The oRPC client: MODULE_OFF; SOURCE_OWNED; a collection writes the Receipt, allocation and party balance atomically, and a forced failure leaves nothing.
  • tenancy.test.ts gains the School procedures and tables.
  • access.test.ts covers the role union across Finance and School, and MODULE_OF.
