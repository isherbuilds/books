Accly Books keeps parties, receipts, files, and organization settings together.
You join an organization through an invitation from its owner; there is no
public sign-up.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/getting-started) explains accounts, invitations, organization URLs, and access.
- [Administration and handover](/administration-and-handover) covers setup, members, files, and audit.

## Choose the correct organization

Each organization has its own URL. Use the organization switcher in
the sidebar when you need to move between organizations. The selected
organization applies only to that browser tab.

## Main areas

- **Receipts** records money received and prints each receipt.
- **Parties** holds customers, vendors, and other counterparties, with each
  party's receipts and ledger.
- **Files** stores private organization documents.
- **Settings** manages the organization, banks, members, and audit history
  according to your role.
