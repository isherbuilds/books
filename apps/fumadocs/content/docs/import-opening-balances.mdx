## When to use this

Use **Settings → Import** for a one-time move from Tally, Excel or another
bookkeeping system. It creates new accounts, parties and items, and can post your
trial balance with the unpaid invoices, bills and unused credits behind it. It
does not bring in your old transaction history or update existing records.

For just a few opening balance lines, use the form in **Settings → Opening
balance** instead.

Only an **Owner** or **Accountant** can use **Import**. A **Chartered Accountant**
or **Operator** does not see this settings page. Your CA can still help prepare
and review the figures before an owner or accountant imports them.

## Before you start

- Agree the cutover date and a balanced trial balance with your CA. India's
  financial year runs from April to March. For a fresh start on 1 April 2026,
  agree the closing balances as at 31 March 2026.
- List each unpaid customer invoice and vendor bill, with its original reference,
  date, amount still unpaid and, if relevant, due date.
- List unused credits and unmatched advances separately for each party. Do not
  deduct them from an invoice or bill and also list them as a credit: that would
  count the same credit twice.
  List only credits not already matched to a specific invoice or bill in your
  old books; if an advance was already set off against an invoice, enter only
  that invoice's remaining amount as the Claim.
- Review the existing chart of accounts. Use its account codes or exact names for
  balances and item income accounts. List only new accounts, parties and items
  in their creation sheets; a name that already exists is an error.
- Confirm no Opening Balance is already posted. A posted Opening Balance blocks
  an import containing a trial balance or opening items.

## Import in six steps

1. **Download template.** Open **Settings → Import** and click **Download
   template**. Keep the sheet names and the headers on row 1 unchanged.
2. **Fill the workbook.** Complete the sheets below, with data starting on row 2.
   Save an Excel workbook (`.xlsx`) of no more than 5 MB, then choose it on the
   import page. Paste values rather than formulas.
3. **Check.** Click **Check**. This writes nothing. If the workbook is valid,
   **Ready to import** shows the record counts, trial balance totals and net
   party balances. Compare them with the figures approved by your CA.
4. **Fix any errors.** The error table gives **Sheet**, **Row**, **Column** and
   **Message**. If there are more than 500 errors, it says “Showing 500 of N
   errors”; fix those and check again to see any remaining errors. After editing
   in Excel, save the workbook, click **Clear**, choose the saved file again and
   click **Check**. If the chosen file changes on disk, the browser refuses to
   re-send it until you choose it again.
5. **Import.** **Import** becomes available only after a clean **Check** of the
   currently chosen file. Choosing another file resets that approval. Click
   **Import** and review **Import workbook?**: “This creates the workbook's
   masters and posts any opening balances. Imported masters cannot be deleted.”
   Confirm only when the figures are right. Any error means nothing is written:
   the import is all or nothing.
6. **Verify.** After **Import complete**, use the **Opening balance** and
   **Parties** links. Check the posted balances and opening items against your
   old books before recording new receipts or payments.

**Items**, **Trial balance**, **Opening items**. Every sheet except **Read me** may
be empty; leave its header row in place. A required column needs a value in each
row you fill. **Opening** has an additional condition described below.

### Trial balance

| Column  | Required               | Accepted values                                                                                | Example                  |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Account | Yes                    | Code or name of an existing active account that takes postings, or one created in **Accounts** | `1300` or `Cash in Hand` |
| Debit   | One of Debit or Credit | Amount above zero; leave blank or zero if Credit is above zero                                 | `8000`                   |
| Credit  | One of Debit or Credit | Amount above zero; leave blank or zero if Debit is above zero                                  | `5000`                   |

Each row must have exactly one amount above zero. Total debits must equal total
credits. **Accounts Receivable** (`1300`) and **Accounts Payable** (`2000`) must
also agree with the party opening items below.

Do not put GST or advance accounts in the trial balance. For a legacy GST balance,
create an account that takes postings, such as `GST payable at cutover` under
`Current Liabilities`, and carry the balance there. Agree its later clearance by
**Journal** with your CA. For customer or vendor advances, include the unused
amount as a **Credit** in **Opening items** and include it in the net control
balance. If your organization has a GSTIN, the trial balance also cannot contain
a taxable income account; agree an appropriate other account with your CA.

### Opening items

A **Claim** is an unpaid invoice or bill. A **Credit** is an unused credit or
unmatched advance that can be applied later. Enter the amount still open at
cutover, not an invoice's original total if it has already been partly paid.

| Column    | Required | Accepted values                                                                       | Example      |
| --------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Party     | Yes      | Name of an existing party or one created in **Parties**                               | `Priya`      |
| Side      | Yes      | `Receivable` for customer amounts; `Payable` for vendor amounts                       | `Receivable` |
| Type      | Yes      | `Claim` or `Credit`                                                                   | `Claim`      |
| Reference | Yes      | Original reference; the combination of Party, Side, Type and Reference must be unique | `INV-88`     |
| Date      | Yes      | Original date, on or before the opening date                                          | `2026-02-10` |
| Due date  | No       | Claims only; on or after Date; may be after the opening date                          | `2026-03-12` |
| Amount    | Yes      | Rupee amount above zero                                                               | `10000`      |

### Cell, amount and date rules

- Use text, numbers or dates. No formulas, spreadsheet errors or rich-text cells;
  paste values only.
- Amounts may be numeric cells or plain text such as `2000.00`. Use at most two
  decimal places and never a negative amount. In text cells, do not type a rupee
  symbol or thousands separators: use `8000.00`, not `₹8,000.00`.
- Dates may be Excel dates or text in `YYYY-MM-DD` form. Use `2026-03-31`, not an
  ambiguous text date such as `03/04/26`.
- Keep each sheet to at most 5,000 data rows. Fully blank rows are skipped.
- Keep the file at or below 5 MB.

## Why the party totals must match

The trial balance gives one total for all customers and one for all vendors.
**Opening items** explains those totals party by party. Importing both does not
count them twice; they must describe the same balances.

- **Accounts Receivable** (`1300`), debit less credit, must equal receivable
  claims less receivable credits.
- **Accounts Payable** (`2000`), credit less debit, must equal payable claims
  less payable credits.

For example, Priya's unpaid invoice of ₹10,000.00 less her unused advance of
₹2,000.00 gives receivables of **₹8,000.00**. Mehta Traders' unpaid bill of
₹5,000.00, with no unused vendor credit, gives payables of **₹5,000.00**.
The trial balance therefore needs ₹8,000.00 debit in `1300` and ₹5,000.00 credit
in `2000`. A control balance with no matching opening items is refused, even if
total debits and credits agree.

### What you see after Import

**Parties** shows Priya at **₹8,000.00 Dr** and Mehta Traders at
**₹5,000.00 Cr**. Dr means a net amount due from Priya; Cr means a net amount
you owe Mehta Traders.

The opening ledger lines are dated **31 Mar 2026**, so choose **Last year
(2025–26)** in the party **Ledger** to see them.

Priya's ledger:

| Document  | Date        | Particulars              | Debit      | Credit    | Balance       |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------------ | ---------- | --------- | ------------- |
| OC25-26/1 | 31 Mar 2026 | Opening invoice · INV-88 | ₹10,000.00 |           | ₹10,000.00 Dr |
| OA25-26/1 | 31 Mar 2026 | Opening credit · ADV-3   |            | ₹2,000.00 | ₹8,000.00 Dr  |

Debits **₹10,000.00**, Credits **₹2,000.00** and Closing **₹8,000.00 Dr**.
For **This year (2026–27)**, it starts with Opening **₹8,000.00 Dr**.

opening bill in the **Payment** open-items grid. An opening credit can be applied
to an open opening invoice or bill with that row's **Apply credit** button in
**Settings → Opening balance**, or to a new **Invoice** or **Bill** for that party
through **More actions** (…) → **Apply credit**.

### Settle the balances after cutover

1. **Receive INV-88 in full.** Open **Receipts → New**, choose **Priya** as the
   Party, enter **10000** as the Amount and select **Against open items** as the
   Settlement kind. The **Open items** grid lists “Opening invoice · OC25-26/1 ·
   INV-88” with Outstanding **₹10,000.00**. Click **Fill**, choose the payment
   method that actually received the money, then **Post**. This creates
   **RCT26-27/1**. In **Settings → Opening balance**, `INV-88` now has Balance
   **₹0.00**. Priya's balance is **₹2,000.00 Cr** — her unused `ADV-3`.
2. **Pay Mehta Traders.** Open **Payments → New**, choose **Mehta Traders** as the
   Party, select **Against open items** and enter Amount **5000**. Click
   **Fill** on `B-17`, choose the payment method actually used, then **Post**.
   This creates **PMT26-27/1**. `B-17` now has Balance **₹0.00**, and Mehta
   Traders' balance is **₹0.00**.
3. **Use ADV-3 on Priya's next invoice.** Open **Invoices → New**, set **Bill
   to** to **Priya**, add item **Tuition** with Rate **3000**, then **Post**.
   This creates **INV26-27/1** for **₹3,000.00**. Open it and choose **More
   actions** (…) → **Apply credit**. In **Credit**, choose “Opening credit ·
   OA25-26/1 · ADV-3 … ₹2,000.00”; Amount fills **2000.00**. Click **Apply
   credit**. You see “Credit applied”, status **Part paid** and Outstanding
   **₹1,000.00**. Under **Allocations**, **OA25-26/1** shows **₹2,000.00**,
   **Active** and a **Reverse** action. `ADV-3` now has Balance **₹0.00**, and
   Priya's balance is **₹1,000.00 Dr**.

Document numbers depend on your organization's prefixes and financial year.
Record receipts and payments on their actual dates. Do not create a second
invoice or bill for the old amounts.

To set an opening credit against an opening invoice instead, open **Settings →
Opening balance** and choose **Apply credit** on the invoice's row. If the advance
was already set off in your old books, the workbook should have shown only the
invoice's remaining amount as the Claim, without listing the same advance as a
Credit.

## Common errors and fixes

Any error stops the whole import; none of the workbook's records is written.
After each Excel correction, save, **Clear**, choose the file again and **Check**.

| Error or symptom                                                                          | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Fix                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trial balance debits and credits differ                                                   | The total debit is not the total credit.                                                                                                                                                                                    | Reconcile the source trial balance with your CA. Do not add an unexplained balancing figure just to pass Check.                                            |
| “Accounts receivable is ₹7,000.00 but the receivable items net to ₹8,000.00.”             | Even a balanced trial balance is wrong if its party control total disagrees. In the example, changing receivables to ₹7,000.00 and capital to ₹22,000.00 still balances overall, but does not match ₹10,000.00 − ₹2,000.00. | Restore the agreed ₹8,000.00 receivables and ₹23,000.00 capital, or correct the source items if those were wrong. Check the payable total in the same way. |
| “Parties · 2 · Name · A party with this name already exists.”                             | Row 2 is trying to create an existing party. The import never updates it.                                                                                                                                                   | Remove that party's creation row. Use the existing party's name in Opening items. For an existing account or item, remove its creation row too.            |
| “Items · 2 · Income account · No active account has the code or name No Such Account.”    | The income account on Items row 2 cannot be found.                                                                                                                                                                          | Use the correct active income account code or name, or create it in Accounts; for this example use `Tuition Fees`.                                         |
| “Enter a due date on or after the date.”                                                  | A claim's Due date is earlier than its Date.                                                                                                                                                                                | Correct the dates from the old invoice or bill. Leave Due date blank on credits.                                                                           |
| “Paste values only: no formulas, errors or formatted text.”                               | A cell contains a formula or another unsupported cell type.                                                                                                                                                                 | Replace it with its plain value using Excel's paste-values option, then save.                                                                              |
| “Enter a non-negative amount with two decimal places at most and no thousands separator.” | An amount has more than two decimals, is negative, or is text containing a separator.                                                                                                                                       | Correct the amount to rupees and paise, such as `2000.00`. Use no commas or rupee symbol in text cells.                                                    |
| “Opening balance [number] is already posted. Cancel it first.”                            | A trial balance or opening items cannot be imported while an Opening Balance is posted.                                                                                                                                     | Review the existing Opening Balance. If it is wrong and nothing is applied to its items, cancel it before importing the replacement.                       |

## Undo an import

**Clear** only clears the chosen workbook and its check result. It does not undo
an import.

To reverse imported opening balances, open **Settings → Opening balance** and
use **Cancel opening balance**. The Opening Balance and all its opening items
cancel together; you cannot cancel a single opening item on its own. Cancellation
reverses the opening balances on the original cutover date, and the original
remains in the audit history.

Cancellation is refused while any receipt, payment or credit is applied against
an opening item. Review those settlements with your accountant before attempting
to reverse the opening balances.

Imported accounts, parties and items **cannot be deleted**, and cancelling the
Opening Balance does not remove them. For a corrected import, leave those
already-created records out of the creation sheets and refer to their existing
names or codes. An import containing only new accounts, parties or items has no
Opening Balance to cancel.
