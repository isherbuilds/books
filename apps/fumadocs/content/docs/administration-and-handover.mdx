## Set up the organization

Follow the in-app **Setup path**:

1. **Organization** — confirm legal name, address, tax identifiers, time zone,
   financial-year start, and document prefixes. These values shape business
   dates and printed document numbers.
2. **Banks** — add each bank account and cash box, then the payment methods
   that land in them, such as UPI, bank transfer, card, cheque, and cash.
3. **Members** — invite each person by email address, then assign the role they
   need:
   - **Owner** — everything. Only an owner manages members, invitations, and
     settings, and uploads or deletes files.
   - **Accountant** — manages parties, bank accounts, and payment methods;
     records and cancels receipts; reads bank balances and the audit history.
   - **Chartered Accountant** — reads everything, including bank balances and
     the audit history; never records or cancels.
   - **Operator** — records receipts and reads parties; never cancels, creates
     parties, or exports.

   The owner can change a person's role, remove them, or revoke a pending
   invitation.

Review identifiers, time zone, and prefixes with the responsible person before
live use. Changing configuration later does not rewrite documents already
issued.

## Files

**Files** stores organization documents privately. Only an owner uploads or
deletes a file. Downloads use signed links that expire after 15 minutes. Treat a
signed link as a temporary bearer link and do not place it in messages or logs.

## Audit

The Audit settings tab records selected sensitive or destructive actions and
permission denials. It is not a history of every read or every ordinary edit.
Use it to investigate who performed a recorded action; use the source record and
financial documents for the business history itself.

## Handover

Accly Books has no in-app reports yet. Each receipt prints as a PDF. Ask the
owner for the day book or TDS register export. Have the pilot's chartered
accountant approve classifications and printed fields before you rely on an
export.
