## Set up the organization

Review **Settings → Organization** to confirm the legal name, address, tax
identifiers, financial-year start, and document prefixes. These values shape
printed document numbers. Business dates use India Standard Time.

Follow the in-app **Setup path**. The steps shown depend on your role:

1. **Add bank accounts and payment methods** — open **Banking** and add each
   bank account and cash box, then the payment methods that land in them, such
   as UPI, bank transfer, card, cheque, and cash.
2. **Enter opening balances** — carry over balances from your previous books
   on the cutover date. Choose one of the routes below.
3. **Review the team** — open **Settings → Members**. The owner invites each
   person by email address and assigns the role they need:
   - **Owner** — everything, including posting and cancelling opening balances
     and importing a workbook. Only an owner manages members, invitations, and
     settings, and uploads or deletes files.
   - **Accountant** — manages parties, bank accounts, and payment methods;
     records and cancels receipts; posts and cancels opening balances; can
     import a workbook; reads bank balances and the audit history.
   - **Chartered Accountant** — reads everything, including opening balances,
     bank balances and the audit history; never records or cancels, and cannot
     import a workbook.
   - **Operator** — records receipts and reads parties; never cancels, creates
     parties, or exports, and cannot post opening balances or import a workbook.

   The owner can change a person's role, remove them, or revoke a pending
   invitation.

The **Settings** tabs are **Organization**, **Members**, **Opening balance**,
**Import**, **Locks**, **Files**, and **Audit**. You see only the tabs your role
allows.

Review identifiers and prefixes with the responsible person before
live use. Changing configuration later does not rewrite documents already
issued.

## Bring in opening balances

Choose one route before you begin recording new transactions:

- **Settings → Import** — an **Owner** or **Accountant** can bring accounts,
  parties, items, a trial balance, and unpaid invoices, bills, and credits in
  from an Excel workbook. **Check** writes nothing. Import is all-or-nothing:
  if there is an error, nothing is written. Follow
  [Move from your old books](/import-opening-balances) for the template, checks,
  and full worked example.
- **Settings → Opening balance** — for a few ledger lines without party
  balances, an **Owner** or **Accountant** can enter the **As at** date and
  debit and credit balances, then choose **Post opening balance**. Debit and
  credit totals must match. Use **Import**, not this form, for unpaid customer
  invoices, vendor bills, or party credits.

For example, start the April–March financial year on 1 April 2026 with balances
as at **31 March 2026**:

| Account             | Debit          | Credit         |
| ------------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| Cash in Hand        | ₹20,000.00     | —              |
| Accounts Receivable | ₹8,000.00      | —              |
| Accounts Payable    | —              | ₹5,000.00      |
| Capital Account     | —              | ₹23,000.00     |
| **Total**           | **₹28,000.00** | **₹28,000.00** |

Priya's unpaid **INV-88** of ₹10,000.00 less her advance **ADV-3** of ₹2,000.00
backs the ₹8,000.00 receivable. Mehta Traders' unpaid bill **B-17** backs the
₹5,000.00 payable. This example needs **Import** so the individual outstanding
items come in with their matching account balances. See the
[full walk-through](/import-opening-balances) to enter the workbook and settle
these items after import.

## Files

**Files** stores organization documents privately. Only an owner uploads or
deletes a file. Downloads use signed links that expire after 15 minutes. Treat a
signed link as a temporary bearer link and do not place it in messages or logs.

## Audit

The Audit settings tab records selected sensitive or destructive actions and
permission denials. It is not a history of every read or every ordinary edit.
Use it to investigate who performed a recorded action; use the source record and
financial documents for the business history itself.

## Handover

Reports opens the trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, account
ledger and day book in the app. Click an account to open its ledger, and a
document number to open the document. Each report downloads as Excel or PDF
for its period, and a party's Ledger tab downloads its statement. The owner,
accountant and CA can read these reports; the operator cannot. Have the
pilot's chartered accountant approve classifications and printed fields
before you rely on a report.
