## Start an appointment

Open **OPD**, choose **New appointment**, and complete the form in order:

1. **Customer** — search by the available customer details and select the correct
   record. Register the customer from the same search when no safe match exists.
2. **Care** — choose the department and practitioner.
3. **When** — choose **Now** for an arriving customer or **Later** for a future
   organization-local date and time.
4. **Services** — add any known billable services. Services are optional in
   both modes; **Now** also shows the configured attendance fee for review.

Accly Books warns before leaving a changed form. Resolve that warning instead of opening
another destination and assuming the draft was saved.

### Now

**Now** uses server time and creates a checked-in appointment. When a billable
line remains, a user with billing access reviews the itemized amount, discount
details, and up to four payment lines before issuing and collecting. When no
attendance fee or selected service remains, Accly Books creates only the appointment and
token; it does not invent an Invoice or Payment.

### Later

**Later** requires a future minute. Selected services are saved at their current
price but remain pending until check-in. Review them again when the customer
arrives.

## Work the day list

The OPD page is a dated list. Search within the selected day, move to another
date when needed, and include closed rows only when you need to inspect them.

- **Booked** — scheduled but not yet arrived. You can check in, reschedule, mark
  no-show, or cancel when your role allows it.
- **Checked In** — the customer arrived. Open the record for the clinical or
  billing work owned by your role.
- **Cancelled** and **No show** — closed outcomes. Pending Charges are not left
  available for billing.

## Use the appointment record

An appointment has two permission-controlled tabs over the same record:

- **Clinical** shows the customer and attendance facts, prints the OPD slip, and
  attaches or downloads the signed prescription scan. The signed paper remains
  the clinical source.
- **Billing** reviews pending Charges, issues Invoices, records collections, and
  shows later corrections.

Continue with [Billing and corrections](/billing-and-corrections) before issuing
or changing a financial document.
