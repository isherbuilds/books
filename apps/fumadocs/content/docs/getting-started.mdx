## Accounts and invitations

Accly Books has no public sign-up. An organization owner invites your email
address and gives you the invitation link directly; no email is sent.

1. Open the invitation link. It creates your account, or asks you to sign in
   if the account already exists.
2. Choose the pending invitation on the **Join** page.
3. Choose **Join**. Accly Books opens that organization's setup page; open
   **Receipts** when setup is complete.

If an invitation has expired or was revoked, ask the owner for a new one.
Creating another account does not restore the invitation.

## Organization URLs

Every organization has its own URL. The organization switcher uses normal links,
so each browser tab can stay in a different organization. Before recording a
receipt, uploading a file, or changing settings, check the organization name in
the sidebar.

## Access and roles

Your role controls which destinations and actions appear. Being able to view a
record does not necessarily allow you to change it, cancel a receipt, manage
members, or delete a file. Ask the organization owner when an expected action
is unavailable; do not work around it in another organization.

## Main destinations

- **Receipts** lists money received and opens each receipt.
- **Parties** finds, registers, and opens parties.
- **Files** and **Settings** support handover and administration.

Continue with the [Administration and handover](/administration-and-handover)
guide for setup and access tasks.
