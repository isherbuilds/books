Most entries in Accly Books come from [documents](/glossary#document), the business
papers you record: an [invoice](/glossary#invoice) (a bill you send a customer)
[posts](/glossary#post) the sale and the [GST](/glossary#gst) (Goods and Services Tax),
and a [receipt](/glossary#receipt) posts the money you receive. To post is to write the
entry into the books for good. Accounting covers the two tools that sit beside those
documents. A [**journal**](/glossary#journal), a manual entry, posts what no document
covers, such as [depreciation](/glossary#depreciation) (the share of an asset's cost
used up in the period) or a [bad debt](/glossary#write-off) (money a customer will
never pay). A [**lock**](/glossary#lock) closes a period to new entries after your CA
(Chartered Accountant) has reviewed it and after you file the GST return.

Find **Journals** under **Accounting** in the sidebar. Find **Locks** under
**Settings → Locks**.

## When to use a journal and when to use a document

Use a document whenever one fits. The document carries the [party](/glossary#party)
(the customer or vendor), the GST and the print, and the reports group it correctly.
Use a journal only for what is left, such as an [accrual](/glossary#accrual) (an
expense or income that belongs to this period but is paid later) or a
[contra](/glossary#contra) entry (money moved between your own cash and bank).

| You want to                                                                            | Use                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bill a customer or record a sale with GST                                              | [Invoice](/sales/invoices)                                             |
| Record a vendor's bill, including bank charges that carry GST                          | [Bill](/purchases/bills)                                               |
| Receive or pay money, with a bank fee                                                  | [Receipt](/sales/receipts) or [Payment](/purchases/payments)           |
| Take a part payment and write off the small rest of the invoice                        | [Receipt](/sales/receipts) with a write-off line                       |
| Reduce an invoice for a return or a discount                                           | [Credit note](/sales/credit-notes)                                     |
| Book depreciation, accruals, bank charges without GST, or a cash deposit into the bank | [Journal](/accounting/journals)                                        |
| Write off a customer's unpaid invoice                                                  | [Journal](/accounting/journals#write-off-a-bad-debt) with a party line |
| Stop anyone from changing a period you have closed                                     | [Lock](/accounting/locks)                                              |

## Who can do what

| Action                       | Owner | Accountant | Chartered Accountant | Operator |
| ---------------------------- | ----- | ---------- | -------------------- | -------- |
| See journals                 | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | No       |
| Post and cancel journals     | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |
| See the locks                | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | No       |
| Set or clear a lock          | Yes   | No         | Yes                  | No       |
| Grant or revoke an exception | Yes   | No         | Yes                  | No       |

Each member's [role](/glossary#roles) decides what they can do. See
[Roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access) for the full table.

## Closing a period, in short

1. Post the period's adjusting journals: depreciation, accruals, bank charges,
   write-offs.
2. Ask your CA to review the [Trial balance](/reports/trial-balance) for the
   period: the list of every account's balance, where the debits and credits must
   agree ([trial balance](/glossary#trial-balance)).
3. File the GST return ([GSTR-1](/glossary#gstr), the monthly or quarterly return of
   your sales) from the GST outward and inward registers on [Reports](/reports).
4. Set the **Books** lock and the **Tax period** lock to the last day of the period.
5. For a late correction, grant a short [exception](/glossary#lock-exception), a
   time-limited permission for one person to post into the locked period. Post the
   correction and let the exception expire.

these steps for April to June 2026.
