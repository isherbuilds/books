---
title: Handover
description: Hand the books to a new owner or chartered accountant, and what to transfer and check
---

A handover passes responsibility for an organization's books to someone else:
a new owner after a change in the business, a new accountant, or a new CA (Chartered Accountant) firm
at the start of an audit. In Accly Books the books themselves never move. You
change **who** has access and **what you give them** to start from.

- **Who does it:** the **Owner**. The outgoing accountant or CA helps prepare
  the reports.
- **When:** at a period end that is agreed with the incoming person, usually a
  month, quarter or [financial year](/glossary#financial-year) end (in India the
  financial year runs April to March, so 31 March).

## Before the handover date

<Steps>
<Step title="Finish the open work">

On **Home**, open **Draft invoices** and **Draft bills** ([bills](/glossary#bill)
are supplier invoices). A [draft](/glossary#draft) is saved but not final.
[Post](/glossary#post) (make final) or delete each draft, so that nothing half-done sits in the books. Record all [receipts](/glossary#receipt) (money in) and
[payments](/glossary#payment) (money out) up to the handover date.

</Step>
<Step title="Check money and party balances">

Open **Banking** and compare each bank and cash balance with the bank statement
and the cash count on the handover date. Open **To collect** and **To pay** on
**Home** and confirm each open invoice and bill with the [party](/glossary#party)
(customer or supplier) where needed.

</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the reports pack">

An owner, accountant or CA opens **Reports** and downloads, for the period up
to the handover date, each as Excel or PDF:

- [Trial balance](/reports/trial-balance): every account's balance, which must
  total equal on both sides ([glossary](/glossary#trial-balance))
- [Profit and loss](/reports/profit-and-loss) (income less expenses for the period)
  and [Balance sheet](/reports/balance-sheet) (what the business owns and owes on
  a date); see the glossary for [P&L](/glossary#profit-and-loss) and
  [balance sheet](/glossary#balance-sheet)
- [Day book](/reports/day-book) for the period: every entry in date order
  ([glossary](/glossary#day-book))
- [Account ledger](/reports/account-ledger), every entry in one account
  ([ledger](/glossary#ledger)), for bank, cash, [GST](/glossary#gst) (Goods and
  Services Tax) and any account the incoming person asks about
- A [party statement](/reports/party-ledger) from the party's **Ledger** tab for each party with a balance

In each report, click an account to open its ledger, and a document number to
open the document. An operator cannot open reports.

</Step>
<Step title="Close the period">

The CA or the owner sets the **Books lock** ([lock](/glossary#lock): a date on or
before which nobody can post) and, once the [GST returns](/glossary#gstr)
(GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) for the period are filed, the **Tax period lock** to the handover date. After that, no
one can post into the handed-over period without a
[lock exception](/glossary#lock-exception), a time-limited permission. An
exception lifts only the Books lock. A document that affects GST still cannot be
posted into a period under the Tax period lock until the CA or owner moves that
lock to an earlier date. See
[Period locks](/accounting/locks).

</Step>
<Step title="Collect the papers">

Upload the supporting papers to **Settings → Files**: bank statements, GST
returns filed, [TDS](/glossary#tds) (tax deducted at source) certificates, loan and rent agreements. Name each file so it
says what it is and which period it covers (see [Files](/administration/files)).

</Step>
<Step title="Review the audit log">

Read **Settings → Audit**, the [audit log](/glossary#audit-log) of sensitive
actions, for the period. Look at every [cancelled](/glossary#reversal) or amended
document and its reason, every lock exception, and any run of **Denied**
entries. Explain anything unusual in your handover note. See
[Audit log](/administration/audit-log).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Hand over to a new owner

The organization must always have at least one owner, so add the new owner
before the old one steps back.

1. In **Settings → Members**, invite the new owner's email address with the
   **Owner** role, and send them the link.
2. The new owner opens the link, creates an account or signs in, and clicks
   **Join**. Ask them to confirm they see **Settings → Organization**.
3. The new owner reviews **Settings → Organization**: legal name, [PAN](/glossary#pan) (Permanent Account Number), address,
   state, financial year and document prefixes. Changes there apply to new
   documents only; they do not rewrite documents already issued.
4. Then either the new owner removes the old owner, or the old owner changes
   their own role, for example to **Chartered Accountant** during the transition. A
   Chartered Accountant reads everything and records no documents, but can
   still change period locks and grant lock exceptions.

If you try to step back while you are the only owner, Accly Books refuses with
"Could not update the roster" and nothing changes.

## Hand over to a new CA or accountant

1. Invite the new person with the **Chartered Accountant** or **Accountant**
   role.
2. After they join and you have checked that they can open the reports,
   remove the outgoing person from **Settings → Members**.
3. Give the new person the reports pack and the handover note.

The outgoing person's documents and audit entries stay in the books after you
remove them.

## Tidy access

- Remove everyone who has left the business.
- Cancel pending invitations nobody will use.
- Check each remaining person's role against
  [Roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
- Never share a sign-in. Each person uses their own account, so the audit log
  shows who did what.

## What does not move

- **Passwords.** Each person sets their own when they join. Accly Books has no
  password reset yet.
- **The organization address.** The `/cedar-components` part of the address is
  permanent.
- **Deleting the organization.** An organization cannot be deleted from the
  app.

Have the incoming CA approve the account classifications (which [group](/glossary#group-account) each
account sits under) and the printed fields
on invoices and receipts before you rely on a report after the handover.
