title: Administration
description: Manage members, store files, read the audit log and hand the books over
---

Administration is the work around the books (the accounting records) rather than in them: who can open
them, where supporting papers live, who did what, and how to pass the books to
someone else. It lives under **Settings** in the sidebar.

| Task                                                       | Where                  | Who                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Invite people, change roles, remove members                | **Settings → Members** | Owner                                   |
| Upload and delete files                                    | **Settings → Files**   | Owner (everyone can open files)         |
| Read who did what                                          | **Settings → Audit**   | Owner, accountant, chartered accountant |
| Hand the books to a new owner or CA (Chartered Accountant) | Several places         | Owner                                   |

**Settings** opens the first tab your role can see. An owner sees seven tabs:
**Organization**, **Members**, **Opening balance**, **Import**, **Locks**,
**Files** and **Audit**. The organization, [opening balance](/glossary#opening-balance) (balances carried
over from your old books), import and [lock](/glossary#lock) (closes past dates to
changes) tabs are covered in [Setup](/setup) and [Accounting](/accounting/locks).
