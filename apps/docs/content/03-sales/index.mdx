Sales covers everything between you and your customers: the [invoices](/glossary#invoice)
you raise, the money you receive ([receipts](/glossary#receipt)), the
[credit notes](/glossary#credit-note) you issue for returns, and the matching of payments
and credits to the invoices they settle ([allocation](/glossary#allocation)).

Find these pages under **Sales** in the sidebar: **Invoices**, **Receipts** and
**Credit notes**.

## How the pieces fit

| You want to                                                                       | Use                                                                      | What it does to the customer's balance                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bill a sale                                                                       | [Invoice](/sales/invoices)                                               | Raises what the customer owes (Dr, a [debit](/glossary#debit-credit))         |
| Take money before you bill                                                        | [Receipt](/sales/receipts), kind **Advance**                             | Holds the money as the customer's [advance](/glossary#advance) (Cr, a credit) |
| Take money for invoices you raised                                                | [Receipt](/sales/receipts), kind **Against open items**                  | Lowers what the customer owes                                                 |
| Use an advance for a new invoice                                                  | [Apply credit](/sales/apply-credit)                                      | No change: it moves the advance onto the invoice                              |
| Take back goods or lower a price                                                  | [Credit note](/sales/credit-notes)                                       | Lowers what the customer owes                                                 |
| Sell at the counter and get paid at once ([counter sale](/glossary#counter-sale)) | [Invoice](/sales/invoices#counter-sale-paid-now) with **Record payment** | Raises and settles in one step                                                |

Every [document](/glossary#document) is a [**draft**](/glossary#draft) (saved but not in your
books; invoices only), [**posted**](/glossary#post) (recorded in your books) or
**cancelled**. A posted document is never edited. To change it, you cancel it, which
posts a [reversal](/glossary#reversal) (an equal and opposite entry), or issue a credit
note. The register keeps the cancelled document for audit.

## Who can do what

| Action                                        | Owner | Accountant | Chartered Accountant | Operator |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------- | -------------------- | -------- |
| See invoices and receipts                     | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | Yes      |
| See credit notes                              | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | No       |
| Create, save and post invoices; post receipts | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | Yes      |
| Cancel or amend invoices; cancel receipts     | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |
| Post and cancel credit notes                  | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |
| Apply credit and reverse an allocation        | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |

These roles are explained in the [glossary](/glossary#roles). An operator who posts a receipt against invoices also makes those matches, because
they come with the receipt. See [Roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Before you start

- Add the items you sell in [Items](/setup/items). Every invoice line is an item: the
  item carries the [HSN or SAC](/glossary#hsn-sac) code (the GST classification code), the
  income [account](/glossary#account) and the [GST](/glossary#gst) rate.
- Add your customers in [Parties](/setup/parties), with [GSTIN](/glossary#gstin) (GST registration number) when they have one. The
  party's state sets the default [place of supply](/glossary#place-of-supply), which decides
  the type of GST.
- Check your [payment methods](/glossary#payment-method) (Cash, [UPI](/glossary#upi-neft), Bank transfer, Card) in
  [Banking](/setup/banking). Each one posts to its own cash or bank account.

For a full month of sales from advance to collection, follow
[A month of trading](/scenarios/trading-month).
