Purchases covers everything between you and your suppliers: the [bills](/glossary#bill)
they send you, the money you pay them, and the [debit notes](/glossary#debit-note) you
raise when goods come back short or a price comes down. Accly Books
[posts](/glossary#post) each of these, that is, records it for good, to the
[ledger](/glossary#ledger), the running record of every account. So your
[payables](/glossary#payable) (what you owe suppliers), your
[input tax credit](/glossary#itc) (ITC, the [GST](/glossary#gst) you paid that you may set against the GST you collect)
and your [TDS Payable](/glossary#tds-payable) ([TDS](/glossary#tds), tax deducted at
source, that you kept back from suppliers and now owe the government) are always up to date.

Find these pages under **Purchases** in the sidebar: **Bills**, **Payments** and
**Debit notes**.

## How the pieces fit

| You want to                                  | Use                                                                                                                                 | What it does to the supplier's balance                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Book a supplier's invoice                    | [Bill](/purchases/bills)                                                                                                            | Raises what you owe (Cr, a [credit](/glossary#debit-credit))       |
| Pay before the supplier bills you            | [Payment](/purchases/payments), kind **Advance**                                                                                    | Holds the money as an [advance](/glossary#advance) to the supplier |
| Pay bills you have booked                    | [Payment](/purchases/payments), [settlement kind](/glossary#settlement-kind) **Against open items**                                 | Lowers what you owe                                                |
| Use an advance against a new bill            | **Apply credit** ([allocation](/glossary#allocation), matching a credit to a bill) on the [bill](/purchases/bills#apply-an-advance) | No change: it moves the advance onto the bill                      |
| Return goods, or the supplier lowers a price | [Debit note](/purchases/debit-notes)                                                                                                | Lowers what you owe                                                |
| Receive a supplier refund                    | [Receipt](/sales/receipts#refund-a-supplier), **Money back from supplier**                                                          | Clears unapplied debit notes or advances without recording income  |
| Pay an expense that has no bill              | [Payment](/purchases/payments#pay-an-expense-directly), **Direct**                                                                  | No change: it goes straight to the expense                         |

## Who can do what

| [Role](/glossary#roles) | Bills                | Payments                             | Debit notes  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------ |
| Owner, Accountant       | Create, post, cancel | Post every kind, cancel              | Post, cancel |
| Operator                | No access            | Post **Advance** and **Direct** only | No access    |
| Chartered Accountant    | View                 | View                                 | View         |

An Operator cannot see bills, so the **Against open items** choice does not appear on
their payment form. See [Roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Before you start

- Record each supplier as a [party](/glossary#party) (anyone you deal with) in
  [Parties](/setup/parties), with the **Vendor** role and its **State**. A bill cannot
  post without the supplier's state: Accly Books uses it to decide between CGST + SGST
  and IGST ([the three parts of GST](/glossary#cgst-sgst-igst): central and state tax
  within one state, integrated tax between states).
- Record the supplier's **PAN** ([Permanent Account Number](/glossary#pan), the
  income-tax ID) before you deduct TDS. A [GSTIN](/glossary#gstin), the 15-character GST
  registration number, carries the PAN inside it.
- Make sure the [chart of accounts](/glossary#chart-of-accounts), your list of
  [accounts](/glossary#account), in [Chart of accounts](/setup/chart-of-accounts) has the expense or asset
  accounts you buy into, such as **Purchases**, **Professional Fees** or **Plant and
  Machinery**.
- Set up a [payment method](/glossary#payment-method) (a named way money moves, tied to
  one bank or cash account) in [Banking](/setup/banking) for each bank account or cash box you pay from.

For a complete example, read the [purchase cycle](/scenarios/purchase-cycle) scenario:
an advance, a goods bill, a consultant's bill with TDS, a debit note, the final payment
and the TDS deposit.
