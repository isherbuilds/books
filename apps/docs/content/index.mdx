Accly Books is accounting and billing software for Indian businesses. You record
sales, purchases and money movements as documents. Each posted document writes its
journal entry for you, so your ledgers, GST and party balances stay in step.

Each guide below shows the real screens with an example. The scenarios follow one
business through a whole task, from start to finish.

## How the books stay correct

- **Documents, not loose entries.** An invoice, receipt, bill or payment posts its own
  journal entry. You use a journal only for adjustments.
- **Posted means final.** You cannot edit a posted document. You cancel it, and
  Accly Books writes the reversing entry on the same accounts.
- **Every organization is separate.** Each organization has its own URL, members,
  numbering and books. The organization switcher moves you between them.
- **Closed periods stay closed.** After your accountant files a return, a lock stops
  anyone from changing that period by mistake.
