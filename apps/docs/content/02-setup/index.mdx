---
title: Set up your books
description: Organization details, accounts, banks, items, parties and opening balances, in the order to set them up
---

Before you raise the first [invoice](/glossary#invoice) (the bill you give a customer for a sale), tell Accly Books who you are, where money is kept,
what you sell, who you deal with, and what your books held on the day you start. Each
step below takes a few minutes. Most organizations do them once and then change them
rarely.

## The order to follow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the organization details">
    Legal name, [GSTIN](/glossary#gstin) (your 15-character GST registration number), address and
    document prefixes print on every [document](/glossary#document). See
    [Organization](/setup/organization).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the chart of accounts">
    Every organization starts with a ready [chart of accounts](/glossary#chart-of-accounts), the
    list of every ledger your books post to. Add the expense and income accounts your business
    needs. See [Chart of accounts](/setup/chart-of-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your bank accounts and payment methods">
    One account for each bank account or cash box, and one [payment
    method](/glossary#payment-method) (a named way money moves, such as UPI) for each way money
    moves. See [Banking](/setup/banking).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your items">
    The goods and services you sell, with [HSN or SAC](/glossary#hsn-sac) (the GST code for goods or
    services), price and [GST](/glossary#gst) rate. See [Items](/setup/items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your customers and vendors">
    Each is a [party](/glossary#party), with GSTIN where they have one. See
    [Parties](/setup/parties).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your opening balances">
    Your [opening balance](/glossary#opening-balance) is what your old books held on the day you
    switch. Type a few balances by hand in [Opening balance](/setup/opening-balance), or import the
    full [trial balance](/glossary#trial-balance) (the list of every account's balance) with unpaid
    invoices and [bills](/glossary#bill) from Excel in [Import opening
    balances](/setup/import-opening-balances).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new organization also opens a short **Setup** page with three of these steps: bank
accounts and payment methods, opening balances, and the team. You can do the steps in
any order and come back later. Only the opening balance needs care: [post](/glossary#post) it (record it in the books) once,
before you rely on your reports.

## Where to find these pages

| Page                    | In the app                         |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Organization            | **Settings → Organization**        |
| Chart of accounts       | **Accounting → Chart of accounts** |
| Banking                 | **Accounting → Banking**           |
| Items                   | **Masters → Items**                |
| Parties                 | **Masters → Parties**              |
| Opening balance         | **Settings → Opening balance**     |

## Who can do what

| Action ([roles](/glossary#roles))               | Owner | Accountant | Chartered Accountant | Operator |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------- | -------------------- | -------- |
| Change organization details and prefixes        | Yes   | No         | No                   | No       |
| See accounts, items, parties and methods        | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | Yes      |
| Add or change accounts, items, parties, methods | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |
| See bank balances on **Banking**                | Yes   | Yes        | Yes                  | No       |
| Post or cancel the opening balance              | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |
| Import a workbook                               | Yes   | Yes        | No                   | No       |

An operator can pick existing parties and items on an invoice or receipt, but cannot
add new ones. See [Roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

To move a running business from Tally, follow the scenario
[Cut over from Tally](/scenarios/cutover-from-tally).
