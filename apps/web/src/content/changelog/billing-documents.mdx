Receipts now render as PDFs from one server-side endpoint. Preview in the
browser, print at the desk and the file a party downloads are the same bytes
from the same source facts.

## What prints

- The Organization's legal name, address and GSTIN, from the snapshot taken
  when the receipt was posted, so a later settings change never rewrites an old
  receipt.
- Party names in Devanagari where that is how they were entered.

## What does not

A receipt is never labelled as the itemized bill; they are different documents
with different requirements. And until the pilot's chartered accountant signs
off on classifications, nothing we print makes a _Tax Invoice_ or _Bill of
Supply_ claim. We would rather print less than print something we cannot yet
stand behind.

## Corrections

A posted receipt never changes. A mistake is cancelled, which reverses its
entries, so the trail shows what happened, not only the final number.
